A NOTE ON LANGUAGE

“The plant wants” is a useful fiction.

A plant does not plan to recruit a bird or bargain with a fungus. But lineages carrying traits that accomplished those things left more descendants. Natural selection can produce an exquisite appearance of purpose without a planner.

Here, playful language opens the door. Evidence decides how far we walk through it.
