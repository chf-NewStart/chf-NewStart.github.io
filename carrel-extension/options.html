Read in Phloem

Let Phloem open local PDFs

Chrome keeps files on your computer behind a separate permission that only you can enable.

  1. Right-click the Read in Phloem extension icon.
  2. Choose Manage extension.
  3. Turn on Allow access to file URLs.
  4. Return to your PDF and click the Phloem icon again.
