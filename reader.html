Read-aloud room 🍅

Drop in a text you own and it reads aloud, one sentence at a time, highlighting as it goes. It runs entirely in your browser with the voices already on your device — nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored anywhere but here.

Your text stays on this device. Reload and it's still here until you clear it.
–

The reading itself uses your browser's built-in speech (the Web Speech API). Which voices you get depends on your device. A cloned version of a specific person's voice isn't something that can run free and offline in a browser, so that isn't offered here — these are the system voices only.
