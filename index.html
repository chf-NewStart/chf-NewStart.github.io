FLAGSHIP RESEARCH · UNIVERSITY OF WATERLOO

Computational research for greenhouse systems

> Active PhD research · public summary only

  • AreaComputational methods for understanding and working with complex greenhouse systems.
  • ApproachCouple greenhouse sensing with crop-growth models and control, so the system can be simulated, diagnosed, and steered — a working twin of the real thing.
  • Why it's hardPartial observability, biological variability, and safety constraints — the settings where estimation and feedback methods earn their keep.
  • DisclosureMethods, parameters, data, figures, and results remain private pending publication and IP review.
  • Next updateA fuller case study will appear here after the research team clears material for public release.

Supervised by Prof. Nasser Mohieddin Abukhdeir and Prof. Christian Euler · Chemical Engineering
