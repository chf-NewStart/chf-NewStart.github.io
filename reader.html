Read-aloud room 🍅

Drop in a .txt or .pdf you own and it reads aloud, one sentence at a time, highlighting as it goes. The file itself never leaves your device. One caveat, stated plainly: the best-sounding voices are your browser's online voices, and those send the sentences to the voice provider (e.g. Google) to be spoken. Pick an on-device voice and nothing leaves at all — it just sounds more robotic.

Your text stays on this device. Reload and it's still here until you clear it.
–

The reading uses your browser's built-in speech (the Web Speech API) and PDF text is pulled out locally with a copy of pdf.js served from this site. Voices marked online are neural and sound natural, but the browser sends each sentence to that provider to be spoken; voices without that mark run fully on your device and leak nothing, at the cost of sounding robotic. A cloned version of one specific person's voice can't run free and offline in a browser, so it isn't here.
