## Promotional text

Give one question your attention. A quiet focus timer, a place to park distractions, and a research notebook that leaves you a clear next step.

## Description

Small steps. Deeper roots.

Tend gives thoughtful work a little room. Begin with one question or task, choose a focus interval, and leave a few words for your future self when you finish.

MAKE AN INTENTION
Choose one small question and an optional stopping point. Keep a single intended weekly result in your notebook.

FIND YOUR RHYTHM
Choose 15/3, 25/5, or 50/10 minutes of focus and rest, or set your own durations. Pause, continue, or finish early. A longer break follows four full intervals. Quiet view keeps the room simple.

PARK A THOUGHT
Capture an idea or distraction without leaving your question. Review it when you have room.

LEAVE A THREAD
Record what you learned, where you are stuck, and the next small action. All reflection fields are optional. Export the week’s notes as Markdown through the iOS share sheet, or keep a full notebook backup.

ROOTED IN SOURCES
Explore concise, linked references to Deep Work, Slow Productivity, the Pomodoro Technique, and research on if–then planning and short breaks. The app distinguishes practical adaptations from research findings. No interval is presented as universally optimal.

AT HOME ON YOUR DEVICE
The timer and notebook work offline. Optional local notifications remind you when a session ends, subject to your iOS notification settings. No account, advertising, analytics SDKs, or AI service. Your notebook is stored locally; you choose when to export it. Device backups follow your platform settings.

Tend is independently developed by Houfu Chen and is not affiliated with the cited authors. It is a work and study tool, not a medical treatment. Timer minutes reflect elapsed sessions, not a measurement of attention or research quality.

## App Review notes

No account, login, server, or sample credentials are needed. Core functionality works offline.

1. On Focus, enter any task and tap Start focusing.
2. Pause/Continue works without losing elapsed time. After at least one second, Finish early & reflect opens a reflection sheet.
3. Add optional notes and choose Save & finish, or Save & start break. Open Notebook to see the session.
4. Park a thought on Focus, then review it in Notebook.
5. Settings offers custom durations, optional local session reminders, backup export/restore, and screen-awake control. Native exports use the iOS share sheet. Restoring a backup asks before replacing current data.
6. Sources gives full attribution, links, and evidence limitations. These are paraphrases and independent adaptations; no book text is reproduced.

The app bundles its interface and content and uses native app-scoped storage, local notifications and share-sheet exports. It is not a remotely hosted website viewer. The privileged bridge only accepts messages from its bundled main frame; source links open externally.
