Phloem for iPad

A little help with your reading desk.

Read your papers, keep a guide on the page, and collect notes beside the original. These instructions cover the iPad app.

Get in touch

Email Houfu Chen at houfuchen0702@gmail.com. If the link does not open a mail app, copy the address into your email service.

For a problem report, include your iPad model, iPadOS version, app version or TestFlight build, what you were trying to do, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help; cover private information first. You do not need to send your papers or backup to make an initial report.

Add a paper

  1. Use the library’s add control and choose a PDF or Word document from Files.
  2. Wait for the import to finish, then open the paper from your library.
  3. Keep the original file somewhere you control. Files from a cloud provider may need an internet connection for the initial import.

If a PDF link cannot be imported, download the PDF with your browser first, then choose that file in Phloem. Some websites block direct downloads or require a login.

Save and restore a complete backup

  1. Open Settings, then choose Save complete backup.
  2. Keep Phloem open while it prepares the file. In the iPad share sheet, choose Save to Files or another destination you trust.
  3. To restore, open Settings → Restore from Files and select the backup.

A complete backup includes original documents and your reading work. Restoring adds separate copies and keeps existing papers. Older metadata-only backups do not contain PDF or Word originals; Phloem asks before restoring those notes.

The current backup limits are 80 MiB of original files in total, 64 MiB per original, 16 MiB of metadata, and 2,000 papers. A missing original stops a complete backup. Re-import that original and try again. For a large library, keep original files separately and contact support before removing papers to reduce its size.

Backups are unencrypted. Store them somewhere appropriate for the contents of your papers and notes. Closing or cancelling the share sheet does not save a backup.

If something is missing or will not open

  • Missing original: re-import the same PDF or Word file from Files. A restored older backup may contain only the notes.
  • Storage warning: free space on your iPad, keep the original files, and try saving a backup again. Avoid deleting or reinstalling Phloem before securing a backup.
  • Export did not finish: close any open share sheet, keep Phloem in the foreground, and retry. If it still fails, note the error and contact support.
  • Definition unavailable: Define uses Wikipedia and Wikimedia online. Try a shorter phrase and check your connection. Your highlights do not depend on a lookup succeeding.

What this iPad build supports

Local reading, notes, highlights, imported reviewer comments, original-file sharing, and complete library backups are available. Cloud AI, Chrome’s on-device AI, and Google Drive/GitHub sync are unavailable. Files saved locally can be read without a network connection; Define, PDF URL imports, external links, and cloud file providers can require one.
