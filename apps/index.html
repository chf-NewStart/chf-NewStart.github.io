Phloem: Paper Reader

A calm reading desk for research papers

iPad · web

On the App Store

A local-first reader for PDFs and Word files. A reading guide walks you down the page, pages turn like paper, and Apple Pencil notes sit beside the source. AI is optional and privacy-first.

Qi Zi: Go, Chess & Mahjong

Four classic games, with a coach

iPhone · iPad · web · Android in the browser

On the App Store · 1.2 out now

Go, Gomoku, chess and Mahjong, each against the computer with a coach that says why. On the 3D boards every Go group grows into a snake or a dragon, and a big capture slays it with a temple bell.

  • New in 1.2: the start screen is the app icon come alive, and Go has famous games to watch and life-and-death problems.
  • Win rates while you play, and a graph that marks the turning points after.
  • Online rooms shared by the app and the browser, up to four at a Mahjong table.
Get it on the App Store ↗ Play in your browser ↗
