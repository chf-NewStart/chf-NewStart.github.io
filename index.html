01 / Research

Computational research for greenhouse systems.

My PhD connects sensing, crop physiology and control in one modelling loop, so a greenhouse can be simulated, diagnosed and steered.

Research · public summary

A working twin of a real greenhouse

Greenhouses are partially observable, biologically variable and safety-critical, which makes them a natural testbed for state estimation, feedback and digital-twin methods.

  • ApproachCouple greenhouse sensing with crop-growth models and control, so the system can be simulated, diagnosed and steered.
  • SupervisorsProf. Nasser Mohieddin Abukhdeir and Prof. Christian Euler, Chemical Engineering
  • DisclosureMethods, data and results stay private until publication and IP review clear them. A fuller case study will appear here then.
Try the idea Grow a Tomato: steer a toy greenhouse twin and race an autopilot → A free browser game built from textbook plant physiology, not my research model.

Phloem: Paper Reader

A calm reading desk for research papers on iPad

On the App Store

A reading guide that walks you down the page, real page turns, and Apple Pencil notes beside the source. New on the web: faster Workspace handwriting, sticky notes on PDF pages, and an export that writes your marks into the PDF. AI is optional and privacy-first.

Why I made itMy professor told me to stop letting AI summarize papers and actually read them. No reader I tried kept me on the page, so I built one. I named it after my PhD: phloem carries sugar from the leaves to the fruit, and a reader carries knowledge from paper to brain. Say it fast and it's "flow them", which is kind of the point.

03 / Selected work

Proof lives in the prototype.

A method matters when it survives contact with reality and leaves its evidence visible.

Routing · open notebook

Smart evacuation maps

Problem
Shortest-path routing can pile crowds into the same exits during large evacuations.
Method
Dijkstra routing, ant-colony-inspired optimization and LLM-assisted decisions in one agent-based framework.
Evidence
Outperformed plain Dijkstra in the tested majority-evacuation scenarios.
GitHub ↗ Build note ↗

Physical computing · light

S.W.I.M., the Sequential Wave Imprinting Machine

Idea
Move a one-dimensional strip of light through space to paint a two-dimensional image.
Build
Arduino, addressable LEDs and long-exposure photography turn digital pixels into a physical trace.
Context
A hardware project sponsored by Prof. Steve Mann.
