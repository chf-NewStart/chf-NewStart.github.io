This is the objective function. FBA finds the flux distribution that maximises whatever you pick here, subject to the carbon and nitrogen the source can deliver. Pick two things that compete and watch one starve the other.

Amino acids sit at 0% under every stage preset, and that is the model being correct rather than broken: none of engine.py's stage objectives reward storing them, so there is no reason to run those reactions. Ask for them here and they climb to about 29%.
