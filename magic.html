Pinch your thumb and finger together in the air and something appears in your hand. Open your hand to let it drop. It all runs on your device — the camera feed never leaves this browser.

Your camera turns on only after you press start, and the video is processed entirely on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Hand tracking runs on a copy of Google's MediaPipe model served from this site (~17 MB, loaded once and cached). It needs a browser with a camera and WebAssembly. On a phone this is a big first load.
